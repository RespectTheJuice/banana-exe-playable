## Five-second recall test

After every major beat, ask a test player to look away and answer one question.

- BANANA reveal: "What weird thing did the job ask for?"
- YOU GOT IT: "What did you just agree to do?"
- Purchase: "What banana did you buy, what did it cost, and what is left?"
- Postal beat: "Why are you not just posting a loose banana?"
- Part 2 analysis: "What are the three constraints?"
- Leicester result: "Where did the banana end up?"

A beat that repeatedly fails recall needs more hierarchy, a longer hold, clearer consequence or player control. More text is not automatically the answer.

## Pacing default

Do not measure pacing only in milliseconds. Use player control at comprehension gates.

- Minor reaction: 1.8–2.3 s can be enough.
- Important consequence with no input: approximately 2.8–3.5 s, depending on reading load.
- Multi-item analysis: player-controlled continuation preferred.
- Never auto-advance directly out of a state whose facts matter to the next decision.
